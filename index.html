About Madinah

Madinah is one of the most holiest places in Islam, a city filled with diversity, history, and spiritual longing. As the destination of the Prophet Muhammad's (PBUH) historic Migration (Hijrah), it became the heart of the early Islamic civilization. Millions of pilgrims and visitors from around the globe gather here every year to experience its tranquility, explore its historical landmarks, and immerse themselves in its rich heritage and welcoming atmosphere.
