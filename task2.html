Favorite Books

  1. الجامع الكامل في الحديث الصحيح الشامل

    The author of this book has attempted to compile all the authentic Ahadith of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ that were previously scattered across different Hadith collections.

    The author was formerly a Hindu from India who accepted Islam and began his Hadith studies at the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah.

    1. This work is very unique and probably unprecedented.
    2. The author's remarkable journey from Hinduism to Islam is truly admirable.
    3. It brings together authentic Ahadith from various collections in one place.

  2. المعجم الاشتقاقي المؤصل

    This is a very handy dictionary, specifically designed to help readers understand the words of the Quran. It explains the roots of Arabic words and their meanings.

    1. It is arranged in a proper dictionary format based on root words.
    2. It mentions the Quranic usage of each root word.
    3. It explains the central meaning of each root, making it easier to understand its different derivatives.

  3. رياض الصالحين

    Compiled by Imam Yahya ibn Sharaf al-Nawawi, this book is a renowned collection of Quranic verses and authentic Ahadith covering various aspects of Islamic life, including worship, manners, sincerity, patience, and good character.

    1. It provides practical guidance for developing good character and Islamic manners.
    2. Its chapters are arranged systematically according to different topics of Islamic teachings.
    3. It contains valuable teachings of the Prophet ﷺ that help a Muslim improve both his religious and daily life.
