Key Attractions in Madinah

Home | Attractions | Hotels

  1. Al-Masjid an-Nabawi (The Prophet's Mosque)

    The central landmark of Madinah and the second holiest site in Islam. Built originally by the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), it features the famous Green Dome and iconic modern parasol umbrellas.

  2. Masjid Quba

    Recognized as the first mosque ever built in Islamic history. Its foundation stone was laid by Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) upon his arrival in Madinah.

  3. Mount Uhud

    A historic mountain north of Madinah, famous for being the site of the Battle of Uhud. It offers historical insights and scenic views of the surrounding area.

  4. Masjid al-Qiblatayn

    Known as the Mosque of the Two Qiblas, this is the historic site where the direction of Islamic prayer (Qibla) was commanded to change from Jerusalem to Mecca.

  5. Dar Al Madinah Museum

    A specialized museum exhibiting models, artifacts, and historical documentation depicting the urban and cultural history of Madinah across different eras.
